One component, every tab: Wishlists · My Trips · Profile · Dashboard · Reservations · Earnings all render through AppPageHeader.
Consistent title: 26px, weight 800, −0.5 letter-spacing, ink color — no more “small vs big”.
Action chips: messages + notification bell now share a soft circular chip (matches the Explore search bar’s rounded icons); the bell keeps its shake + unread badge.
Tabbed screens (Trips, Reservations) drop the chunky Material AppBar+TabBar for the same flat header with a slim tab strip — and the earlier Android double-gap stays fixed (header adds no SafeArea).
