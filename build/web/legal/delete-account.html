Delete your account

Last updated 26 August 2026

You can have your Musaafir account and its data deleted. You do not need the app installed to ask, and you do not need to be able to log in.

How to request it. Email support@musaafir.app with the subject Account deletion request, from the email address on your account if you have one. Include the mobile number you sign in with. That number is your account identifier and we cannot find your account without it.

We will confirm the request, verify that it is really you — normally by a one-time code to that number, so that nobody can delete someone else's account — and complete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, email address, photo, and biography.
  • Your host verification documents — national ID images, selfie, and any proof-of-address document — and the ID number itself.
  • Your listings and their photos.
  • Your saved payout methods.
  • Your push notification tokens, so notifications stop.
  • Your account credentials. You will not be able to log in again, and a future sign-up with the same number starts a fresh, empty account.

What we keep, and why

A small amount of data outlives the account, because deleting it would break someone else's records or leave us unable to meet an obligation:

  • Booking, payment, and payout records — retained for financial and tax purposes, and so the other party to a booking keeps their own history. Stripped of your profile details where we can.
  • Reviews you wrote — kept on the listing, anonymised, so a listing's rating history stays honest. Ask us and we will remove the text as well.
  • Messages — a copy remains in the other participant's conversation, as it would in any messaging app.
  • Records we must keep to resolve an open dispute, or where fraud or abuse is under investigation. These go once that concludes.

Backups roll off on their own schedule, so a copy may persist in backup for up to 30 days after deletion.

Deleting some data without deleting the account

If you would rather keep the account, you can edit or remove most of your data yourself in the app under Profile — your photo, biography, listings, and payout methods. To have specific data removed that the app does not let you remove, email us and say which.

Before you ask

  • Hosts: settle or cancel any upcoming bookings first. Deleting your account does not cancel a booking a guest has already paid for.
  • Guests: if you have an upcoming stay, deleting your account cancels your access to it and to the host's messages.
