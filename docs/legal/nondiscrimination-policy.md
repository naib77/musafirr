# Nondiscrimination Policy

**Musaafir** · Last updated 30 August 2026

Musaafir exists so that anyone in Bangladesh can find a place to stay — for
treatment, for an exam, for work, for travel. That fails if who you are
decides whether a door opens. This policy binds every host and every guest on
the platform, and forms part of our [Terms & Conditions](terms-conditions.md).

**The short version.** Hosts decide *how* they host — price, rules, dates.
They do not get to decide *who deserves shelter* based on religion, ethnicity,
caste, gender, disability, or the other characteristics below. A guest turned
away for who they are gets a full refund and our help finding another stay;
the host answers for it.

## 1. Protected characteristics

No host or guest may refuse, cancel, rate down, charge differently, impose
different rules on, harass, or insult another person because of their:

- religion or religious practice
- ethnicity, race, or skin colour
- indigenous identity
- caste
- national origin
- gender
- sexual orientation or gender identity
- age
- disability or illness
- marital or family status — including being unmarried ("bachelor"), being a
  single woman, or travelling with children
- pregnancy
- occupation or lawful source of income

## 2. What hosts may not do

- Decline, cancel, or discourage a booking because the guest has (or the host
  assumes) a protected characteristic. "No bachelors", "families only",
  "no [ethnicity/religion]" — whether written in the listing or applied
  silently — all violate this policy.
- Charge more, demand extra deposits, or apply stricter house rules to some
  guests than others on those grounds.
- Use slurs, stereotypes, or demeaning language about anyone, in listings,
  reviews, or chat.
- Refuse a guest's mobility aid, or charge a disabled guest extra for
  assistance their stay requires.
- Decide *for* the guest that a place "isn't suitable for someone like them".
  Describe the property accurately — stairs, shared bathroom, no lift — and
  let the guest decide what suits them.

## 3. What hosts may do

Legitimate hosting decisions are not discrimination:

- **Shared space may be restricted by gender.** A seat in a shared room, or a
  room sharing a bathroom or kitchen with the host's household or other
  guests, may honestly be offered to one gender only. The restriction must be
  stated in the listing and applied consistently — it is a property of the
  *space*, never a judgement of a *person*. A whole, self-contained unit has
  no such excuse.
- **State facts about the property** so guests can judge suitability
  themselves: "third floor, no lift", "squat toilet only", "the host family
  lives on site".
- **Apply lawful requirements, stated factually.** Where a legal or building
  requirement genuinely applies — for example, verifying a guest's national ID
  — a host may say so plainly and without commentary.
- **Decline for real, non-protected reasons**: dates unavailable, party size
  exceeds the listing's capacity, a guest's own conduct on the platform.

## 4. Guests too

Guests owe hosts the same respect: no demanding a host of a particular
religion or ethnicity, no abusive language about who a host is, no
review-bombing a listing because of the host's identity. A review may judge
the stay; it may not judge the host's existence.

## 5. If it happens to you

Report it — from the listing, the conversation, or the booking in the app, or
to **support@musaafir.app**. If you arrive and are refused the stay because of
who you are, that is a failure to provide access under the
[Refund & Cancellation Policy](refund-policy.md) §4: you get a **full
refund**, and we will help you find another stay for the same dates where we
can.

## 6. Enforcement

A person reviews every report. Depending on what we find, we correct the
listing, warn the host or guest, remove the listing, or remove the account —
and repeat or flagrant violations end accounts, not just listings. What we
never do is quietly ignore it.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top changes when we do, and for a
significant change we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
