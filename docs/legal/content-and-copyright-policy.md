# Content & Copyright Policy

**Musaafir** · Last updated 30 August 2026

This policy covers everything users publish on Musaafir — listings, photos,
reviews, profiles, and messages — and what to do when content is stolen,
false, or abusive. It forms part of our
[Terms & Conditions](terms-conditions.md), which also set out the licence you
give us to host and display what you post.

## 1. Rules for all content

Content on Musaafir must be yours to post, true, and safe. You may not post
content that:

- **you have no right to post** — someone else's photos, text, or personal
  information;
- **misrepresents a stay** — photos that are not of the actual property,
  amenities it does not have, a location it is not at;
- **is discriminatory** — see the
  [Nondiscrimination Policy](nondiscrimination-policy.md);
- **harasses, threatens, or endangers anyone**, or exposes their private
  information;
- **is sexually explicit**, gratuitously violent, or promotes illegal
  activity;
- **games the platform** — fake reviews, reviews traded or paid for, review
  threats ("remove the charge or I rate you one star"), or manipulated
  ratings;
- **moves users off-platform** for the purposes our
  [Terms & Conditions](terms-conditions.md) §6 prohibits.

Reviews carry one extra rule: a review must be about the stay — what was
promised, what was delivered, how people behaved. It is not a venue for
disputes about money (use [support](mailto:support@musaafir.app)), for the
host's or guest's identity (see the Nondiscrimination Policy), or for events
unrelated to the booking.

## 2. Reporting content

Every listing, review, and conversation can be reported from inside the app,
and you can block another user. For anything the app cannot report, email
**support@musaafir.app**. A person reviews every report; we may edit nothing —
we either leave content up, remove it, or remove it and act on the account
that posted it.

## 3. Copyright: when your work appears without permission

The most common case: your listing photos appearing on someone else's
listing. Whether the work is a photo, text, or anything else you own, send a
notice to **support@musaafir.app** with the subject **"Copyright complaint"**
containing:

1. **What is yours** — the original work, and something that shows it is
   yours (the original files, the earlier listing, the source with you named);
2. **Where the copy is** — a link to the listing, review, or profile on
   Musaafir carrying it;
3. **Your contact details** — name, phone number, email;
4. **Two statements**: that you genuinely believe the use is unauthorised,
   and that your notice is accurate and you are the owner or authorised to act
   for them;
5. **Your signature** — typed full legal name is fine.

We review, and where the notice holds up we remove the material and tell the
person who posted it why.

## 4. If your content was removed

If you believe a removal was a mistake — you own the work, or you were
licensed to use it — reply to the removal notice or email us with evidence.
A person reviews it; content that turns out to be legitimate goes back up.

## 5. Repeat infringers

An account that repeatedly posts others' work, fake reviews, or prohibited
content loses features and then loses the account. Fabricating a copyright
complaint to sabotage a competing listing is treated exactly the same way.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top changes when we do.
