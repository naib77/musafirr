# Terms & Conditions

(`co.iobytes.musafir`) and the website — operated by iObytes, Dhaka,
Bangladesh. By creating an account you agree to them.

**What Musaafir is.** A marketplace. We connect guests looking for a stay with
hosts who have one. The stay itself is an agreement between the guest and the
host. We are not the host, the landlord, or the agent, and we do not own or
manage any property listed.

## 1. Your account

- You must be 18 or older.
- You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code. Keep access to that
  number secure — anyone who can receive your codes can reach your account.
- Give accurate information. Impersonating someone else, or listing a property
  you have no right to list, will get your account removed.
- One person, one account. Do not share it.

## 2. If you are a guest

- A booking request becomes a booking when the host accepts it, or immediately
  where the listing is set to book instantly.
- Pay what the booking says, by the method the booking offers. Some hosts
  accept cash on arrival; where they do, that is between you and the host.
- Treat the property with care. You are responsible for damage you cause.
- Read the listing's own rules before you book. They bind you once you book.

## 3. If you are a host

- Only list property you own or are authorised to sublet, and make sure doing
  so is lawful where the property is.
- Describe it honestly. Photos must be of the actual property. Location,
  price, facilities, and availability must be accurate and kept current.
- Honour bookings you accept. Repeated cancellations harm guests and will
  affect your standing on the platform.
- You may be asked to verify your identity, and where our settings require it,
  to prove you control the address, before your listing can go live.
- You are responsible for your own taxes, permits, and any other legal
  obligation attaching to letting your property.

## 4. Payments, payouts, refunds

- Online payments are processed by SSLCommerz. Card and wallet details are
  entered on their page, not ours.
- Payment is due after the host accepts and before the stay ends; a host can
  only mark a stay complete once it is paid.
- Where cash payment is enabled, the guest pays the host directly and we are
  not party to that transaction.
- Host earnings are paid out to the payout method on your account. Payout
  timing depends on the booking's status and on your payment provider.
- Cancellations and refunds are governed by the
  [Refund & Cancellation Policy](refund-policy.md), and in large-scale
  emergencies by the
  [Major Disruptive Events Policy](major-disruptive-events-policy.md). Both

To report something you cannot report in the app, email
**support@musaafir.app**.
